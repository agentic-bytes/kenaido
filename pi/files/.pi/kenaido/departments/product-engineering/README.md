# Product and Engineering

Builds the product, the company's main way of serving people (`ETH-1`). It holds the Scrum Teams, with their Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers.

- **Leadership accountability:** CTO and CPO, always a person (`ORG-3`).
- **Its product:** the company's product. This department holds the Scrum Team that builds it, or the Scrum Teams when it is scaled across several (`SCALE-2`).

## Interface (`ORG-4`)

- **Offers:** Product Increments; architecture and technical advice; estimates and forecasts from flow data; AI evaluations; documentation.
- **A request must contain** (`ORG-5`): the item it serves, the question, the inputs, the expected output with its format and length, when it is needed, and the token budget.
- **Service level expectation:** acknowledged within 1 working day, and consultations answered within 2 working days. This is a starting value, to be replaced by one based on flow data (`FLOW-4`).
- **How to engage it:** through a board item addressed to this department (`ORG-6`), or directly to one of its roles (`ORG-5`).
