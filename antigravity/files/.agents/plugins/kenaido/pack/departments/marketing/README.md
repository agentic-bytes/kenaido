# Marketing

Makes the product known and understood, honestly (`ETH-8`): brand, names, messages, campaigns, discoverability, and community.

- **Leadership accountability:** CMO, always a person (`ORG-3`).
- **Its product:** none of its own yet. Its work is on the company's product backlog, and it holds no Scrum events of its own until its own team is started.

## Interface (`ORG-4`)

- **Offers:** Brand and naming; messages and campaigns; search and discoverability; community.
- **A request must contain** (`ORG-5`): the item it serves, the question, the inputs, the expected output with its format and length, when it is needed, and the token budget.
- **Service level expectation:** acknowledged within 1 working day, and consultations answered within 2 working days. This is a starting value, to be replaced by one based on flow data (`FLOW-4`).
- **How to engage it:** through a board item addressed to this department (`ORG-6`), or directly to one of its roles (`ORG-5`).
