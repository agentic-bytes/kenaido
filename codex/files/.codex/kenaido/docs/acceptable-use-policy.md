# Acceptable-use policy

**Version 1.3.**

## Why this policy exists

kenaido is built to serve people well: fairly, honestly, and without exceptions. Not every use of a coding tool serves people. This policy says, in plain terms, which uses of kenaido — the pack, its extensions, and kenaido's makers' services — kenaido's makers will not build for, sell to, or knowingly support. It is not copied from another company's policy; it comes directly from kenaido's own values.

**Where it binds:** kenaido is under the Apache License 2.0, which lets anyone use it for any purpose, so this policy cannot forbid anyone a use of the free code. For people using it, this is a statement of how kenaido's makers conduct themselves: what they will not build, sell, or knowingly support. It binds a client of kenaido's makers' services, or a customer of any paid offering, only through that client's or customer's contract, where it is a term.

## The order that decides close cases

Values rank in a fixed order: human life first, human dignity second, and the joy of human life third, all standing on a protected environment. When a use is unclear, it is judged against that order: a use that could cost life or safety is refused even if it would be convenient or profitable; a use that is merely unpleasant but harms nobody's safety or dignity is not automatically refused.

## Uses not supported

1. **Anything that could cost human life or safety.** No knowingly building, selling, or supporting software meant to harm people, or used where a failure would put lives at risk without the human safeguards that risk requires.
2. **Deceiving or manipulating people.** No use that tricks people into choices they would not otherwise make (a "dark pattern"), hides material terms, or presents false or unsupported claims as fact.
3. **Taking people's control away.** No use that makes decisions about a person — hiring, credit, access to a service, and similar — without a human able to see, question, and reverse that decision.
4. **Unfair treatment.** No use built or knowingly deployed to discriminate against people, or that kenaido's makers have not checked for unfair bias before it reaches them.
5. **Surveillance without consent.** No use that watches, profiles, or tracks people without their knowledge, beyond what the service they asked for requires.
6. **Breaking the law that applies to the customer.** No knowingly supporting a use that is illegal where it runs.
7. **Reckless waste of computing resources.** No use whose purpose is to run agents at a scale far beyond what the task needs, since the environment is the precondition every other value depends on.

## What this does not cover yet

This policy names the uses that are not supported; it does not yet set out enforcement (how a breach is found or a customer relationship ends), export or sanctions screening, or terms specific to any paid offering. Those follow if paid offerings are ever decided, and get their own review before publication.

## Status

Changes to this policy are decided by the accountable people, with the reasoning kept in the project's own record.
