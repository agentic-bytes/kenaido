# Customer Success

Makes sure customers succeed with the product, and brings what they learn back into the market loop (`ORG-12`).

- **Leadership accountability:** CRO, always a person (`ORG-3`).
- **Its product:** none of its own yet. Its work is on the company's product backlog, and it holds no Scrum events of its own until its own team is started.

## Interface (`ORG-4`)

- **Offers:** Onboarding; support; customer health; customer feedback to Strategy and Product.
- **A request must contain** (`ORG-5`): the item it serves, the question, the inputs, the expected output with its format and length, when it is needed, and the token budget.
- **Service level expectation:** acknowledged within 1 working day, and consultations answered within 2 working days. This is a starting value, to be replaced by one based on flow data (`FLOW-4`).
- **How to engage it:** through a board item addressed to this department (`ORG-6`), or directly to one of its roles (`ORG-5`).

## Roles

**Written and available:**

None yet.

**Defined but not staffed** — engage through `ORG-7`. The role file is written from the template when a person approves staffing:

| Role | What it does | Impact measure (`TEAM-16`) |
|------|--------------|----------------------------|
| Customer Success Manager | Onboarding, adoption, renewals, and customer health | Retention, adoption, and satisfaction |
| Support Specialist | Answers, the knowledge base, and triage of problems to Product and Engineering | Time to resolution, satisfaction, and fewer repeat questions |

## Rules, best practice, and tools

- **Department rules:** none yet. With the first one, this folder gets a `CLAUDE.md` that loads them only when working here (`ORG-14`).
